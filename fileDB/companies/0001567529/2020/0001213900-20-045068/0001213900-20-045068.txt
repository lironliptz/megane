<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: center"><B>Kamada Ltd.</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Kamada Ltd. (the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;)
(NASDAQ &amp; TASE: KMDA), a plasma-derived biopharmaceutical company, today announced that on December 29, 2020, the Company,
the Employees&rsquo; Committee of Kamada&rsquo;s Beit Kama production facility in Israel (the &ldquo;Employee&rsquo;s Committee&rdquo;)
and the Histadrut - General Federation of Labor in Israel (the &ldquo;Histadrut&rdquo;), signed a Memorandum of Understanding (the
&ldquo;MoU&rdquo;) detailing the understandings reached between the parties with respect to severance remuneration for the employees
who will be laid-off as part of the workforce down-sizing planned for 2021 (the &ldquo;Severance Terms&rdquo;) as a result of the
transfer of Glassia&reg; manufacturing to Takeda.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">The signed MoU is intended to form the
basis of a special collective bargaining agreement related to the Severance Terms, which is expected to be signed among the parties
within the next 30 days. In accordance with the terms of the MOU, subject to the execution of such agreement, the previously declared
labor dispute will be terminated.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">For additional details, see the Company&rsquo;s Report on Form
6-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on December 15, 2020.</P>
