<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0.75pt 0pt 0; text-align: right"><B>EXHIBIT 99.1</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><FONT STYLE="background-color: white">Kamada Ltd. (the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;)
(NASDAQ &amp; TASE: KMDA), a plasma-derived biopharmaceutical company, today announced that on December 14, 2020, during the course
of the Company&rsquo;s negotiations with the Histadrut - General Federation of Labor in Israel (the &ldquo;Histadrut&rdquo;) and
</FONT>the Employees&rsquo; Committee of Kamada&rsquo;s Beit Kama production facility in Israel (the &ldquo;Employee&rsquo;s Committee&rdquo;),
on severance remuneration for employees who may be laid-off as part of the workforce down-sizing planned for 2021 (the &ldquo;Severance
Terms&rdquo;) as a result of the transfer of Glassia<SUP>&reg;</SUP> manufacturing to Takeda, the Employee&rsquo;s Committee elected
to declare a labor dispute.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">In the event that the labor dispute will not be resolved within
15 days of its declaration, the Employee&rsquo;s Committee may take further actions in the form of work sanctions and/or work stoppage.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">The Company, the Employee&rsquo;s Committee and the Histadrut
are in advanced negotiations regarding the Severance Terms, and the parties have not yet finalized an agreement.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">Kamada cannot currently predict how the dispute will develop,
whether additional actions will be taken by the Employee&rsquo;s Committee of the Histadrut, or whether the labor dispute will
have an effect on the Company&rsquo;s financial results.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">The Company does not anticipate that the current events will
have an effect, on its 2020 financial results.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>
