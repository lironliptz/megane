Dear Shareholder:
 
On September 19, 2019, Kamada Ltd. (the “Company”) announced the 2019 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders of the Company to be held on November 4, 2019 (the “Meeting”), and furnished the Notice and Proxy Statement for the Meeting and the accompanying proxy card, on Form 6-K, to the Securities and Exchange Commission and the Israel Securities Authority.
 
On September 26, 2019, Brosh Capital Partners, L.P. and Exodus Management Israel Ltd. (together with their affiliates, the “Brosh Group”), which beneficially owned approximately 6.7% of the Company’s ordinary shares as of such date, submitted to the Company a request in accordance with Section 66(b) of the Israeli Companies Law, 1999 (the “Israel Companies Law”) to include on the agenda for the Meeting two proposals: (i) a proposal to determine that the number of directors to serve as members of the Company’s Board of Directors shall be seven (“Brosh Proposal #1”); and (ii) a proposal to elect seven directors out of the nine director nominees proposed by the Company’s Board of Directors for re-election at the Meeting (“Brosh Proposal #2” and together with Brosh Proposal #1, the “Brosh Capital Proposals”).  In addition, the Brosh Group requested that seven out of the nine director nominees be elected by the affirmative vote of the holders of the majority of the ordinary shares represented at the Meeting, in person or by proxy, entitled to vote and voting on the matter, by way of a plurality of votes cast (i.e., that the seven nominees who receive a majority vote and who also receive the largest number of ”FOR” votes will be elected) (the “Plurality Voting Standard”).
 
On October 2, 2019, the Company announced that its Board of Directors has elected to delay the Meeting to allow additional time to communicate with the Brosh Group about the proposals it submitted for the Meeting.
 
The Company subsequently held discussions with the Brosh Group with respect to the Brosh Capital Proposals; however, the Brosh Capital Proposals have not been withdrawn by the Brosh Group to date.
 
Accordingly, the Board of Directors shall convene the 2019 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders of the Company on Tuesday, December 24, 2019, at such time and place, and for such purposes detailed in the enclosed Notice and Proxy Statement for the Meeting and the proxy card for use in connection with the Meeting.
 
Under Section 66(b) of the Israeli Companies Law, one or more shareholders who severally or jointly hold at least 1% of the outstanding voting rights are entitled to request that the Board of Directors include a proposal at a shareholder meeting, provided that such proposal is appropriate for consideration by shareholders at the general meeting.
 
Given that the Brosh Group holds more than 1% of the Company’s voting rights, the Board of Directors discussed and considered the Brosh Proposals to determine, in accordance with Israeli law, if they are appropriate for inclusion on the agenda for the Meeting.
 
With respect to Brosh Proposal #1 to reduce the number of directors who serve on the Board from nine to seven, our Board of Directors determined that the proposal is appropriate for inclusion on the agenda for the Meeting.  Under the Company’s Articles of Association, as currently in effect (the “Articles”), the number of directors of the Company shall be determined from time to time by the annual general meeting, provided that it is not less than five and no more than 11.  Accordingly, the agenda for the Meeting has been updated to include Brosh Proposal #1.

 
While discussing such proposal, members of the Board of Directors expressed their belief that the current Board of Directors effectively and efficiently provide oversight of management’s operation of the Company, in view of the size and complexity of its operations, its current strategic considerations and the transition the Company’s business is currently undergoing. Under the leadership of the current Board of Directors and management team, the Company is focusing on its strategy to create value for the Company and its shareholders.  Accordingly, six out of the eight directors who were present at the Board meeting regarding the Brosh Proposals voted in favor of the resolution to reaffirm the Board’s recommendation to re-elect the nine currently-serving directors. The two remaining directors, Mr. Asaf Frumerman and Ms. Efrat Makov, both of whom had originally voted in favor of the re-election of such nine directors in conjunction with the original Meeting scheduled for November 4, 2019, abstained from voting on the reaffirmation of such resolution.  Accordingly, our Board of Directors recommends that the nine currently-serving directors be re-elected to serve as directors of the Company at the Meeting.
 
In addition, the Board of Directors, together with the Company’s advisors, considered Brosh Proposal #2 to elect seven directors from the nine director nominees proposed by the Board of Directors for re-election at the Meeting,  by way of the Plurality Voting Standard.  Under the Articles, directors are elected by majority vote regardless of whether the election is contested.  Under the majority voting standard, a director nominee is elected if he/she receives greater than 50% of the votes cast.  The Articles do not determine the voting mechanics that would apply in the context of a contested election (i.e., an election in which the total number of nominees for election to the Board of Directors at a general meeting of shareholders, including any director nominees proposed by shareholders in compliance with the requirements of the Israeli Companies Law, exceeds the total number of directors to be elected at such meeting).  As a result, under the Articles, in the event of a contested election of directors, it is possible that more than the maximum number of directors to be elected would be elected to the Board of Directors.  Accordingly, our Board of Directors is of the opinion that the Brosh Group’s request that seven out of the nine director nominees be elected by way of a Plurality Voting Standard is not consistent with the majority voting standard set forth in the Articles.  Furthermore, the Brosh Group request that the directors be elected by way of the Plurality Voting Standard does not address other requirements of Israeli law regarding the composition of our Board of Directors.  As a result of such review and consideration, six out of the eight directors who were present at the Board meeting regarding the Brosh Proposals determined that Brosh Proposal #2 in its current form is not appropriate for inclusion on the agenda for the Meeting.  The two remaining directors, Mr. Frumerman and Ms. Makov, abstained from voting on the resolution of the Board of Directors to determine whether Brosh Proposal #2 is appropriate for inclusion on the agenda for the Meeting and the process that would apply with respect to the election of seven directors if Brosh Proposal #1 shall be approved by the shareholders at the Meeting, as described below.
 
If Brosh Proposal #1 is approved by the shareholders at the Meeting and the number of directors to serve on the Board shall be reduced to seven, the Board of Director’s proposal that the nine currently-serving directors be re-elected to serve as directors of the Company at the Meeting and all votes voted on such proposal will be deemed cancelled.  In such case, no directors will be elected at the Meeting and in accordance with the Articles and applicable law, the nine currently-serving directors shall continue to serve as directors until their successors are duly elected by the shareholders of the Company.  In such event, the Board of Directors will promptly reconvene following the Meeting to consider and determine the appropriate mechanism for the nomination and appointment of the seven directors and thereafter, the Board of Directors will promptly convene a general meeting of shareholders of the Company for the election of the seven directors. If Brosh Proposal #1 is not approved by the shareholders at the Meeting, the Board of Director’s proposal that the nine currently-serving directors be re-elected to serve as directors of the Company at the Meeting will be in effect and votes voted on such proposal will be counted for purposes of such proposal.
 
Shareholders of record at the close of business on November 18, 2019 are entitled to notice of and to vote at the Meeting.
 
Accordingly, enclosed please find the Notice and Proxy Statement for the Meeting and accompanying proxy card for the Meeting.  The proxy card previously distributed by the Company is no longer valid, and any previously distributed proxy card that is delivered to the Company shall not be deemed valid.
 
Whether or not you plan to attend the Meeting, it is important that your ordinary shares be represented and voted at the Meeting. Accordingly, please sign, date and mail the enclosed proxy card in the envelope provided, in accordance with the instructions on your proxy card.
