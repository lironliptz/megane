<div style="FONT-SIZE: 11pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Appoints Senior Biopharmaceutical Executive Michal Ayalon, Ph.D., as Vice </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 11pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">President of Research and Development</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold">Rehovot, Israel &#8211; January 30, 2019</font> &#8211; Kamada Ltd. (NASDAQ &amp; TASE: KMDA), a plasma-derived protein therapeutics company, announced today the appointment of Michal Ayalon, Ph.D., as Vice President of Research and Development.&#160; Dr. Ayalon has nearly 20 years <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">of experience in drug discovery and drug development, including non-clinical and clinical development, manufacturing and control, regulatory, and project management.&#160; </font>She will <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">oversee all of Kamada's R&amp;D and IP activities, and</font> report directly to Amir London, Kamada&#8217;s Chief Executive Officer<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">.</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Dr. Ayalon will succeed Liliana Bar, Ph.D.&#160;who has served as Kamada&#8217;s VP R&amp;D since 2012 and is retiring next month.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#8220;We are excited to have Michal join the Kamada executive team as we begin 2019,&#8221; said Mr. London. &#8220;Michal&#8217;s comprehensive knowledge and understanding of drug development from drug discovery and early-stage research all the way through advanced clinical programs will be of significant value to Kamada as we continue developing our robust pipeline<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">.&#160; I would like to also take this opportunity to thank Liliana Bar for her significant contribution to Kamada leading our R&amp;D and IP activities during recent years.&#8221;</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Prior to joining Kamada, Dr. Ayalon served as Head of R&amp;D at </font>89bio Ltd., where she led the overall development strategy of the company and managed all R&amp;D functions, including medical, clinical, pre-clinical, CMC, regulatory, and project management.<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif"> Previously</font>, Dr. Ayalon was a Project Champion at Teva Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd., where she led novel biologics and biosimilar projects in oncology, respiratory and metabolic disease. Dr. Ayalon&#160; was also Vice President of Research &amp; Development at Galmed Pharmaceuticals Ltd. where she led the pre clinical as well as CMC activities and managed the clinical operation group.&#160; Prior to this, Dr. Ayalon worked for Immune Pharmaceuticals, Inc., BioLineRx and Compugen Ltd.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Dr. Ayalon&#160; received her B.Sc., M.Sc. and Ph.D. from</font> Tel-Aviv University, Faculty of Life Sciences. She completed her postdoctoral research at Weizmann Institute of Science in the Department of Molecular Biology of the Cell.&#160; Dr. Ayalon is the author of multiple patents and publications.</div>
