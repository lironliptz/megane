<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: center; FONT: bold 12pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Kamada Announces Positive Scientific Advice Response from the European </div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: center; FONT: bold 12pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Medicines Agency Focused on Alpha-1 Antitrypsin IV for Treatment&#160;of Acute </div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: center; FONT: bold 12pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Graft-Versus-Host Disease</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Company Received Important Guidance on European Phase 2/3 Study Design and Regulatory Pathway</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold">NESS ZIONA, Israel &#8211; January 23, 2017</font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif"> &#8211; Kamada Ltd. (NASDAQ and TASE: KMDA), a plasma-derived protein therapeutics company focused on orphan indications,&#160;announced today the positive Scientific Advice response&#160; from the Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) of the European Medicines Agency (EMA) focused on the Company&#8217;s development program in Europe for </font>Alpha-1 Antitrypsin (G1-AAT IV) for the treatment of acute Graft-Versus-Host Disease (aGvHD) with<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">&#160;</font>lower gastrointestinal involvement .&#160; The response from the CHMP included important guidance related to the design of the Company&#8217;s planned Phase 2/3 European study and the regulatory pathway for approval based on conducting such a study.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada is in the process of reviewing the guidance received and, following discussions with the Company&#8217;s European Scientific Advisory Board, intends to submit a Clinical Trial Authorization (CTA) application to the EMA in 2017 in order to conduct the Phase 2/3 study.&#160; Importantly, the design of the Company&#8217;s U.S. Phase 2/3 clinical trial of G1-AAT IV in aGvHD, which was recently initiated and is being conducted in the U.S. in collaboration with Shire plc, supports the guidance received from the EMA.&#160; Kamada intends to conduct the European study in parallel with the U.S. study.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#8220;A substantial unmet need remains globally for the treatment of severe aGvHD, especially intestinal aGVHD,&#8221; said Prof. Mohamad Mohty, Head of the Clinical Hematology and Cellular Therapy Department at the Saint-Antoine Hospital in Paris and President of the European Society for Blood and Marrow Transplantation (EBMT). &#8220;The advent of G1-AAT IV as a potential therapy for gut aGVHD is great news. The stem cell transplant community is looking forward towards investigating this agent.&#8221;</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#8220;I would like to thank the EMA for their critical guidance around our European development program for G1-AAT IV for the treatment of aGvHD,&#8221; said Amir London, Kamada&#8217;s Chief Executive Officer.&#160; &#8220;We are especially pleased that the EMA&#8217;s guidance is supported by the trial design being utilized in the U.S. Phase 2/3 study.&#160; Following discussions with our European Scientific Advisory Board, Kamada looks forward to submitting a CTA in 2017 to conduct a European Phase 2/3 study.&#8221;</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Kamada holds all rights to its G1-AAT IV in Europe, and the product is currently available there to acute GvHD patients in need on a named-patient basis through an Early Access Program.&#160; </font>G1-AAT IV previously received orphan drug designation from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the EMA for the treatment of GvHD.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">About Graft-vs-Host Disease</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Graft vs Host Disease, also called GvHD, is a condition that occurs when donor bone marrow or cells attack the recipient.&#160; It is a rare disease, with about 20,000 cases reported in the US and EU per year.&#160; GvHD can occur at any time after a transplant.&#160; However, it is more common after the marrow has started to make healthy cells.&#160; The condition can be mild or severe.&#160; Symptoms vary based on how long someone has had the condition, but may include mouth ulcers, abdominal pain, and rash.&#160; Treatment includes medication to suppress the immune system, such as steroids.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">About Kamada</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Ltd. is focused on plasma-derived protein therapeutics for orphan indications, and has a commercial product portfolio and a robust late-stage product pipeline. The Company uses its proprietary platform technology and know-how for the extraction and purification of proteins from human plasma to produce Alpha-1 Antitrypsin (AAT) in a highly-purified, liquid form, as well as other plasma-derived Immune globulins.&#160; AAT is a protein derived from human plasma with known and newly-discovered therapeutic roles given its immunomodulatory, anti-inflammatory, tissue-protective and antimicrobial properties. The Company&#8217;s flagship product is GLASSIA&#174;, the first and only liquid, ready-to-use, intravenous plasma-derived AAT product approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Kamada markets GLASSIA&#174; in the U.S. through a strategic partnership with Baxalta (now part of Shire plc) and in other counties through local distributors.&#160; In addition to GLASSIA&#174;, Kamada has a product line of seven other pharmaceutical products administered by injection or infusion, that are marketed through distributors in more than 15 countries, including Israel, Russia, Brazil, India and other countries in Latin America and Asia. Kamada has five late-stage plasma-derived protein products in development, including an inhaled formulation of AAT for the treatment of AAT deficiency for which a MAA was submitted to the EMA after completing a pivotal Phase 2/3 clinical trials in Europe. <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">&#160;</font>Kamada has also completed its<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">&#160;</font>Phase 2 clinical trials in the U.S for the treatment of AAT deficiency with inhaled AAT. In addition, Kamada's intravenous AAT is in development for other indications such as type-1 diabetes, GvHD and prevention of lung transplant rejection. Kamada also leverages its expertise and presence in the plasma-derived protein therapeutics market by distributing more than 10 complementary products in Israel that are manufactured by third parties.</div>
