<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: right; FONT: bold 10pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif"><u>Exhibit 99.1</u></div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: right; FONT: bold 10pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Announces New Supply Agreement with International Organization </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">for <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">KamRAB for Post-Exposure Prophylaxis Against Rabies Infection</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Three-Year Agreement Expected to Provide Revenues of Approximately $13 Million to Company</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada&#8217;s Rabies Immune Globulin Recently Received FDA Approval</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold">Rehovot, Israel &#8211; November 21, 2017 </font>-- Kamada Ltd. (NASDAQ and TASE: KMDA), a plasma-derived protein therapeutics company focused on orphan indications, announced today that it has signed a supply agreement with an undisclosed international organization for <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">KamRAB [rabies immune globulin (Human)].&#160; Kamada&#8217;s Rabies Immune globulin recently received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval </font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">for passive, transient post-exposure prophylaxis of rabies infection, when given immediately after contact with a rabid or possibly rabid animal.&#160; This three-year agreement will extend through 2020, and is expected to generate </font>revenues&#160;for Kamada of approximately $13 million.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#8220;We are extremely pleased to begin monetizing KamRAB, leveraging the FDA approval received earlier this year,&#8221; said <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">Amir London, Kamada&#8217;s Chief Executive Officer.&#160; &#8220;We look forward to commencing activities under this supply agreement, with the first shipment already confirmed.&#160; This agreement is an example of the additional commercial potential for this product in international markets, beyond the U.S., and we intend to continue pursuing additional commercial contracts in key strategic territories.&#8221;</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada currently manufactures multiple Immunoglobulin (IgG) products.&#160; The Company intends to continue leveraging its advanced, state-of-the-art, FDA-approved IgG technology to generate new IgG-related business and grow international sales.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">About Kamada</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Ltd. is focused on plasma-derived protein therapeutics for orphan indications, and has a commercial product portfolio and a robust late-stage product pipeline. The Company uses its proprietary platform technology and know-how for the extraction and purification of proteins from human plasma to produce Alpha-1 Antitrypsin (AAT) in a highly-purified, liquid form, as well as other plasma-derived Immune globulins.&#160; AAT is a protein derived from human plasma with known and newly-discovered therapeutic roles given its immunomodulatory, anti-inflammatory, tissue-protective and antimicrobial properties. The Company&#8217;s flagship product is GLASSIA&#174;, the first liquid, ready-to-use, intravenous plasma-derived AAT product approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Kamada markets GLASSIA&#174; in the U.S. through a strategic partnership with Baxalta (now part of Shire plc) and in other counties through local distributors.&#160; In addition to GLASSIA&#174;, Kamada has a product line of seven other pharmaceutical products administered by injection or infusion, that are marketed through distributors in more than 15 countries, including Israel, Russia, Brazil, India and other countries in Latin America and Asia. Kamada has five late-stage plasma-derived protein products in development, including an inhaled formulation of AAT for the treatment of AAT deficiency. In addition, Kamada's intravenous AAT is in development for other indications, such as type-1 diabetes, GvHD and prevention of lung transplant rejection. Kamada's rabies immune globulin (Human) product received FDA approval for Post-Exposure Prophylaxis against rabies infection in August 2017. Kamada also leverages its expertise and presence in the plasma-derived protein therapeutics market by distributing more than 10 complementary products in Israel that are manufactured by third parties.</div>
