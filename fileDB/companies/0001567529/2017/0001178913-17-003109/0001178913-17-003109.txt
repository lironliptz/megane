<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: right; FONT: bold 10pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif"><u>Exhibit 99.1</u></div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Announces Nomination of Three New Members to its Board of Directors</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold">Rehovot, Israel &#8211; November 10, 2017 </font>-- Kamada Ltd. (NASDAQ and TASE: KMDA), a plasma-derived protein therapeutics company focused on orphan indications, announced today that the Company Board of Directors has nominated three new directors to join the Company&#8217;s Board. The new nominees <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">will stand for election at Kamada&#8217;s 2017 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders on November 30, 2017 (the &#8220;AGM&#8221;).</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">The nominees</font>&#160;<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">are Dr. </font>Itzhak Krinsky, Ph.D., Mr. Shmuel (Milky) Rubinstein, and Mr. <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Asaf Frumerman.</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Dr. Krinsky currently serves on the boards of multiple public and private biopharmaceutical companies.&#160; Prior to his retirement as a senior executive, Dr. Krinsky held multiple management-level positions at Teva Pharmaceuticals over a 12 year-period, including Chairman of Teva Japan and Teva South Korea. He also previously served on Teva&#8217;s Executive Committee and as Executive Vice President &#8211; Head of Corporate Business Development.&#160; Dr. Krinsky&#8217;s numerous accomplishments at Teva included the completion of multiple transactions, most notably the acquisitions of Ivax Corporation, Barr Pharmaceuticals and RatioPharm.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Mr. Rubinstein served as Chief Executive Officer of Taro Pharmaceuticals from 1990 to 2010. During this time, the company grew from $10 million in revenue to over $400 million.&#160; He currently serves on the boards of multiple public and private biopharmaceutical companies, including Clal Biotechnology and Exalenz Bioscience Ltd.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">The nomination of Mr. <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Asaf Frumerman</font> is pursuant to a Cooperation Agreement (the&#160; &#8220;Agreement") entered between the Company and one of its largest shareholders, Brosh Capital Partners L.P. and certain of its affiliates (the "Brosh Group"). <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">&#160;</font>Under the terms of the Agreement, the Brosh Group is subject to certain customary standstill and other provisions. The complete Agreement will be included as an exhibit to a Report on Form 6-K, which will be filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Mr. Frumerman has been a Partner at Brosh Capital since 2013 and has been involved with some of the firm&#8217;s largest investments.&#160; Prior to Brosh Capital, Mr. Frumerman worked as an analyst at The Dragon Variation Fund and as a consultant at Ernst &amp; Young (Israel).</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#8220;We look forward to welcoming each of these distinguished individuals to our Board,&#8221; said Leon Recanati, Chairman of the Kamada Board of Directors.&#160; &#8220;Dr. Krinsky, and Messrs. Rubinstein and Frumerman, which were agreed with the Brosh group, will add extensive experience to our Board in the key areas of business development, finance and operations. Moreover, both Dr. Krinsky and Mr. Rubinstein have significant biopharmaceutical industry experience that we intend to leverage as we continue to grow our business. I look forward to working closely with our entire Board and company management as we focus on driving value for all stakeholders, including patients, partners and shareholders.&#8221;</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Additionally, Saadia Ozeri, who has served as a director on Kamada&#8217;s Board since May 2016, has informed the Company of his decision to not stand for re-election at the upcoming AGM in order to focus on other business commitments.&#160; Following this action, and if the three new director nominees are approved during the AGM, Kamada&#8217;s Board will increase to 10 members.</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Ltd. is focused on plasma-derived protein therapeutics for orphan indications, and has a commercial product portfolio and a robust late-stage product pipeline. The Company uses its proprietary platform technology and know-how for the extraction and purification of proteins from human plasma to produce Alpha-1 Antitrypsin (AAT) in a highly-purified, liquid form, as well as other plasma-derived Immune globulins.&#160; AAT is a protein derived from human plasma with known and newly-discovered therapeutic roles given its immunomodulatory, anti-inflammatory, tissue-protective and antimicrobial properties. The Company&#8217;s flagship product is GLASSIA&#174;, the first liquid, ready-to-use, intravenous plasma-derived AAT product approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Kamada markets GLASSIA&#174; in the U.S. through a strategic partnership with Baxalta (now part of Shire plc) and in other counties through local distributors.&#160; In addition to GLASSIA&#174;, Kamada has a product line of seven other pharmaceutical products administered by injection or infusion, that are marketed through distributors in more than 15 countries, including Israel, Russia, Brazil, India and other countries in Latin America and Asia. Kamada has five late-stage plasma-derived protein products in development, including an inhaled formulation of AAT for the treatment of AAT deficiency. In addition, Kamada's intravenous AAT is in development for other indications, such as type-1 diabetes, GvHD and prevention of lung transplant rejection. Kamada's rabies immune globulin (Human) product received FDA approval for Post-Exposure Prophylaxis against rabies infection in August 2017. Kamada also leverages its expertise and presence in the plasma-derived protein therapeutics market by distributing more than 10 complementary products in Israel that are manufactured by third parties.</div>
