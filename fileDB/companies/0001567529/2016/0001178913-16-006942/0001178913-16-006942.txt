<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: right; FONT: bold 10pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif"><u>Exhibit 99.1</u></div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Announces Collaboration Agreement with Yissum for Development of a </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Recombinant Human Alpha 1 Antitrypsin</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Goal of Collaboration is to Maximize Protein Yields and Functionality</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold">NESS ZIONA, Israel &#8211; November 15, 2016 &#8211; Kamada Ltd. (NASDAQ and TASE: KMDA), </font>a plasma-derived protein therapeutics company focused on orphan indications,&#160;announced today that it has signed a collaboration agreement with Yissum Research Development Company of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">for the </font>development of an efficient and robust eukaryotic expression system for recombinant human Alpha 1 Antitrypsin (rhAAT).&#160; The goal of this development work is to maximize protein yields and functionality.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">The collaboration will be led by </font>Dr. Tsafi Danieli, Head of the Protein Expression Facility at Hebrew University.&#160; Dr. Danieli has a vast amount of experience in recombinant DNA technologies and protein production systems.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">&#8220;We are pleased to be working with the Hebrew University on the development of a recombinant human Alpha 1 Antitrypsin, as they are experts in </font>recombinant DNA technologies and protein production systems,<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">&#8221; said Amir London, Kamada&#8217;s Chief Executive Officer.&#160; &#8220;We initiated our development activities in the rhAAT field several years ago in preparation for future increased demand for AAT resulting from greater awareness of AAT deficiency, as well as potential additional indications for Alpha 1 Antitrypsin, which are currently in clinical development. Upon completion of the current work with Yissum, we intend to begin GMP-manufacturing scale up activities.&#160; Our collaboration with Yissum is intended to maintain our innovative global leadership in the area of AAT development in multiple indications.&#8221;</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#8220;The development of an effective recombinant human Alpha 1 Antitrypsin is an important undertaking and would address a significant unmet therapeutic development need,&#8221; said Yaacov Michlin, President and CEO of Yissum.&#160; &#8220;We are excited to collaborate with Kamada on this key project, as our work will result in expanding the range of new technologies and analytical methods essential for <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">the development of </font>therapeutic proteins.&#8221;</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Kamada has previously </font>developed analytical methods (<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">physicochemical</font>, biochemical, in-vitro, and in-vivo) that will help identify and characterize functional rhAAT.&#160; In addition, the Company has established a significant understanding of a favorable expression system and growth conditions required to successfully develop an effective rhAAT.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div id="DSPFPageBreakArea" style="MARGIN-BOTTOM: 10pt; CLEAR: both; MARGIN-TOP: 10pt">
<div id="DSPFPageBreak" style="PAGE-BREAK-AFTER: always">
<hr style="BORDER-LEFT-WIDTH: 0px; HEIGHT: 2px; BORDER-RIGHT-WIDTH: 0px; BORDER-BOTTOM-WIDTH: 0px; COLOR: #000000; CLEAR: both; MARGIN: 4px 0px; BORDER-TOP-WIDTH: 0px; WIDTH: 100%; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #000000" noshade="noshade">
</div>
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">About Kamada</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: justify; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Ltd. is focused on plasma-derived protein therapeutics for orphan indications, and has a commercial product portfolio and a robust late-stage product pipeline. The Company uses its proprietary platform technology and know-how for the extraction and purification of proteins from human plasma to produce Alpha-1 Antitrypsin (AAT) in a highly-purified, liquid form, as well as other plasma-derived Immune globulins.&#160; AAT is a protein derived from human plasma with known and newly-discovered therapeutic roles given its immunomodulatory, anti-inflammatory, tissue-protective and antimicrobial properties. The Company&#8217;s flagship product is GLASSIA&#174;, the first and only liquid, ready-to-use, intravenous plasma-derived AAT product approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Kamada markets GLASSIA&#174; in the U.S. through a strategic partnership with Baxalta (now part of Shire plc) and in other counties through local distributors.&#160; In addition to GLASSIA&#174;, Kamada has a product line of seven other pharmaceutical products administered by injection or infusion, that are marketed through distributors in more than 15 countries, including Israel, Russia, Brazil, India and other countries in Latin America and Asia. Kamada has five late-stage plasma-derived protein products in development, including an inhaled formulation of AAT for the treatment of AAT deficiency for which a MAA was submitted to the EMA after completing a pivotal Phase 2/3 clinical trials in Europe. <font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">&#160;</font>Kamada has also completed its<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">&#160;</font>Phase 2 clinical trials in the U.S for the treatment of AAT deficiency with inhaled AAT. In addition, Kamada's intravenous AAT is in development for other indications such as type-1 diabetes, GvHD and prevention of lung transplant rejection.
