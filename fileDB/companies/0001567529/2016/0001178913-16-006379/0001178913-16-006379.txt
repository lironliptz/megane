<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: right; FONT: bold 10pt/1.25 'Times New Roman', Times, serif"><u>Exhibit 99.1</u></div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada Meets Primary Endpoint of U.S. Phase 2 Study of Inhaled Alpha-1 </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Antitrypsin for the Treatment of Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Deficiency</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold; TEXT-ALIGN: center; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Company&#8217;s Inhaled AAT Demonstrated Significant Increase in Endothelial Lining Fluid </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Inhibitory Capacity</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Study Results Also Showed that Inhaled AAT is the Most Efficient Way of Delivering </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Therapeutic Amounts of AAT to the Primary Sites of Potential Lung Injury</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Kamada to Utilize Results to Design Pivotal U.S. Study and Support Responses to European </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">Medicines Agency (EMA) Regarding Company&#8217;s Marketing Authorization Application </div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-STYLE: italic; TEXT-ALIGN: center; MARGIN-LEFT: 18pt; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">(MAA) for Inhaled AAT</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-WEIGHT: bold">NESS ZIONA, Israel -- August 30, 2016 --</font>&#160;<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Kamada Ltd. (Nasdaq: KMDA) (TASE: KMDA.TA), a plasma-derived protein therapeutics company focused on orphan indications, today announced positive top-line results, meeting the primary endpoint of the Company&#8217;s </font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">U.S. Phase 2 clinical trial of its proprietary inhaled Alpha-1 Antitrypsin (AAT) therapy for the treatment of Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Deficiency (AATD)</font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">.</font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">&#160;</font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">AATD is an orphan disease currently treated by intravenous AAT augmentation therapy.</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">The U.S. Phase 2 clinical trial was a double-blind, placebo-controlled study evaluating the safety and efficacy of AAT by inhalation in 36 AATD patients. Patients were treated with Kamada&#8217;s AAT for inhalation (80 mg/day or 160 mg/day) or placebo via the eFlow&#174; device for 12 weeks during the double-blind period. Primary efficacy measures included antigenic AAT levels and </font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">Anti-Neutrophil Elastase inhibitory</font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff"> (ANEC) levels in the lung as well as additional </font>anti-proteolitic and anti-inflammatory<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff"> biomarkers. Following this double-blind period, eligible patients (total of 26) entered an additional 12-week open-label extension study with the active drug (160mg/day) to further assess safety and tolerability.</font><font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; BACKGROUND-COLOR: #ffffff">&#160;</font></div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">AATD patients treated with Kamada&#8217;s inhaled AAT demonstrated a significant increase in endothelial lining fluid (ELF) AAT antigenic level compared to the placebo group [median increase 4551 nM, p<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">-</font>value&lt;0.0005 (80 mg/day, n=12), and 13454 nM, p<font style="FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif">-</font>value&lt;0.002 (160mg/day, n=12)]. These results are more than twice the increase of ELF antigenic AAT level (+2600 nM) observed in Kamada&#8217;s previously completed intravenous (IV) AAT pivotal study (60mg/kg/week). Antigenic AAT represents the total amount of AAT in the lung, both active and inactive.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>
