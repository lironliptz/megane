<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">Kamada has been selling the product since 2003 in numerous territories outside the U.S. under the brand name KamRAB&#8482;.&#160; Kamada has sold more than one million vials of the product to date, demonstrating significant clinical and safety experience with the product.</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">&#8220;We are pleased to achieve this important milestone in the development of our human rabies immunoglobulin,&#8221; said Amir London, Chief Executive Officer of Kamada.&#160; &#8220;In the U.S., <font style="FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">approximately 40,000 post-exposure prophylaxis treatments are administrated each year, representing an annual market opportunity of over $100 million.&#160; We believe we have a safe and effective product that, if approved, has the potential to gain meaningful market share over time.</font>&#8221;</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">&#160;</div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">&#8220;Kedrion is pleased to be collaborating on the clinical development and anticipated commercialization of an important new rabies treatment here in the United States,&#8221; said Paolo Marcucci, President and Chief Executive Officer of Kedrion S.p.A. &#8220;We have recognized that a clear need exists for an additional rabies treatment option. Kedrion will be making a significant contribution to the health and welfare of large numbers of people who may have been exposed to a life-threatening but completely treatable condition.&#8221;</div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">The BLA is based on results announced in December 2015 from a prospective, randomized, double-blind, non-inferiority Phase 2/3 study of 118 healthy subjects.&#160; The study evaluated pharmacokinetic (PK) parameters of anti-rabies IgG levels in serum at different time points and assessed whether the human rabies IgG interferes with the development of endogenous antibodies. Study results showed that the primary endpoint of non-inferiority was met with a difference of -1.8 percent between the two therapies with variability between -8.2 percent and 3.1 percent (90 percent confidence limit).&#160; No drug-related Serious Adverse Events (SAEs) were experienced by study subjects.</div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">Kamada and Kedrion expect a regulatory decision from the FDA on the BLA in mid-2017, and plan on launching the product soon after a favorable decision.</div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt; FONT-WEIGHT: bold">About Kamada</div>

<div style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25"><br style="LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25">
</div>

<div style="TEXT-ALIGN: left; LINE-HEIGHT: 1.25; FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">Kamada Ltd. is focused on plasma-derived protein therapeutics for orphan indications, and has a commercial product portfolio and a robust late-stage product pipeline. The Company uses its proprietary platform technology and know-how for the extraction and purification of proteins from human plasma to produce Alpha-1 Antitrypsin (AAT) in a highly-purified, liquid form, as well as other plasma-derived Immune globulins.&#160; AAT is a protein derived from human plasma with known and newly-discovered therapeutic roles given its immunomodulatory, anti-inflammatory, tissue-protective and antimicrobial properties. The Company&#8217;s flagship product is Glassia&#174;, the first and only liquid, ready-to-use, intravenous plasma-derived AAT product approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Kamada markets Glassia&#174; in the U.S. through a strategic partnership with Baxalta (formerly Baxter International Inc.&#8217;s BioScience business and now part of Shire plc) and in other counties through local distributors.&#160; In addition to Glassia&#174;, Kamada has a product line of seven other pharmaceutical products administered by injection or infusion, that are marketed through distributors in more than 15 countries, including Israel, Russia, Brazil, India and other countries in Latin America and Asia. Kamada has five late-stage plasma-derived protein products in development, including an inhaled formulation of AAT for the treatment of AAT deficiency that its MAA was submitted to the EMA after completing a pivotal Phase 2/3 clinical trials in Europe. <font style="FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">&#160;</font>Kamada has also completed its<font style="FONT-FAMILY: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; FONT-SIZE: 10pt">&#160;</font>Phase 2 clinical trials in the U.S. In addition, Kamada's intravenous AAT is in development for other indications such as type-1 diabetes, GvHD and prevention of lung transplant rejection. Kamada also leverages its expertise and presence in the plasma-derived protein therapeutics market by distributing more than 10 complementary products in Israel that are manufactured by third parties.</div>
