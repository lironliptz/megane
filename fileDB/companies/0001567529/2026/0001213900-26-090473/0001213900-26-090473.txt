<DESCRIPTION>KAMADA DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.17 PER SHARE; THE COMPANY REPORTED RECORD-HIGH REVENUE AND PROFITABILITY FOR FIRST SIX MONTHS OF 2026
<TEXT>
<HTML>
<HEAD>
     <TITLE></TITLE>
</HEAD>
<BODY STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif">

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: right"><B>Exhibit 99.1</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: center"><B>Kamada Declares Cash Dividend of $0.17 Per Share;
The Company Reported Record-High Revenue and Profitability for First Six Months&nbsp;of&nbsp;2026 </B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: center"><B>Dividend to be Paid Consistent with the Company&rsquo;s
Cash Dividend Policy </B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><B>REHOVOT, Israel, and HOBOKEN, NJ &ndash; August
17, 2026 &ndash;</B> Kamada Ltd. (NASDAQ: KMDA; TASE: KMDA.TA), a global biopharmaceutical company with a portfolio of marketed products
indicated for rare and serious conditions and a leader in the specialty plasma-derived therapies field, today announced that its Board
of Directors has declared a cash dividend of $0.17 (approximately NIS 0.50) per share on the Company&rsquo;s ordinary shares (totaling
approximately $9.8 million). The cash dividend will be payable on September 17, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business
on August 27, 2026. The dividend payment is consistent with the Company&rsquo;s dividend policy.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&ldquo;Based on the Company&rsquo;s record-high
financial results for the first six months of 2026 and our solid cash position, we are pleased to announce a cash dividend to be paid
to our shareholders,&rdquo; said Amir London, Kamada&rsquo;s Chief Executive Officer. &ldquo;This dividend payment reinforces our confidence
in the Company&rsquo;s business prospects and liquidity to continue investing in our commercial growth and securing new business development
and M&amp;A transactions, while also paying dividend to our shareholders. I would like to thank our shareholders for their continued support
and trust in Kamada.&rdquo;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">The Company will withhold tax on the dividend
in accordance with Israeli tax laws. The Company has applied for a ruling from the Israel Tax Authority in connection with tax withholding
to non-Israeli shareholders and will announce the main terms of such ruling once obtained.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><B>About Kamada</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Kamada Ltd. (the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;) is a global
biopharmaceutical company with a portfolio of marketed products indicated for rare and serious conditions and a leader in the specialty
plasma-derived therapies field. FIMI Opportunity Funds, the leading private equity firm in Israel, is the Company&rsquo;s controlling
shareholder, beneficially owning approximately 38% of the outstanding ordinary shares. The Company&rsquo;s strategy is focused on driving
profitable growth through four primary growth pillars: First, organic growth of its commercial portfolio, including continued investment
in the commercialization and life cycle management of its proprietary products, consisting of six FDA-approved specialty plasma-derived
products: KEDRAB&reg;, GLASSIA&reg;, CYTOGAM&reg;, VARIZIG&reg;, WINRHO SDF&reg; and HEPAGAM B&reg;, as well as KAMRAB&reg;, and two equine-based
anti-snake venom products. Second, distribution of third-parties pharmaceutical products in Israel &amp; the MENA region through in-licensing
partnerships, including the launch of several biosimilar products in Israel. Third, the Company is ramping up its plasma collection operations
to support revenue growth through the sale of normal source plasma to other plasma-derived manufacturers, and to support its increasing
demand for hyper-immune plasma. The Company currently owns three FDA-approved operating plasma collection centers in the United States,
in Beaumont, Houston, and San Antonio, Texas. Fourth, the Company aims to secure new mergers and acquisitions, business development, in-licensing
and/or collaboration opportunities, which are anticipated to enhance the Company&rsquo;s marketed products portfolio and leverage its
financial strength and existing commercial infrastructure to drive long-term profitable growth. The Company is leveraging its manufacturing,
research and development expertise to advance the development and commercialization of additional product candidates, targeting areas
of significant unmet medical need.</P>
