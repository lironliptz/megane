<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><B>Change in Dividend Payment Date to April 7, 2026</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">On March 11, 2026, Kamada Ltd. (the &ldquo;<U>Company</U>&rdquo;
or &ldquo;<U>Kamada</U>&rdquo;) announced that its Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.25 (approximately NIS 0.77) per share
on the Company&rsquo;s ordinary shares (totaling approximately $14.4 million), in accordance with the Company&rsquo;s newly adopted annual
cash dividend policy. The dividend was originally scheduled to be paid on April 6, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business
on March 23, 2026.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">The Company hereby announces that the dividend
payment date has been changed to April 7, 2026. All other terms of the previously announced dividend distribution remain unchanged.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">For additional information regarding the cash
dividend, see Exhibit 99.1 to the Company&rsquo;s Form 6-K, furnished to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on March 11, 2026.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>
