<DESCRIPTION>KAMADA ANNOUNCES FDA APPROVAL OF ITS NEW IN-HOUSE RABIES VIRUS NEUTRALIZATION TESTING LABORATORY
<TEXT>
<html>
<head>
     <title></title>
</head>
<body style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif">

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: right"><b>Exhibit 99.1</b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: right"><b>&nbsp;</b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: left"><img src="ea029288601_ex99-1img1.jpg" alt=""></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center">&nbsp;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><b>Kamada Announces FDA Approval of its New In-House
Rabies Virus Neutralization Testing Laboratory </b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><b>&nbsp;</b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><b>REHOVOT, Israel, and HOBOKEN, NJ &ndash; June
1, 2026</b> -- Kamada Ltd. (NASDAQ: KMDA; TASE: KMDA.TA), a global biopharmaceutical company with a portfolio of marketed products indicated
for rare and serious conditions and a leader in the specialty plasma-derived therapies field, today announced FDA approval of the Company&rsquo;s
new in-house Rapid Fluorescent Focus Inhibition Test (RFFIT) laboratory. RFFIT is the gold standard neutralizing test used to measure
the level of rabies-neutralizing antibodies.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">The new laboratory is part of Kamada&rsquo;s Quality
Control laboratory network and represents a significant expansion of the Company&rsquo;s advanced testing capabilities for Anti-Rabies
potency testing involved in the manufacture of KEDRAB<sup>&reg;</sup> and KAMRAB<sup>&reg;</sup>. The new laboratory is located in Kamada's
plant in Beit Kama, Israel, and is one of a limited number of laboratories worldwide which can perform such tests. The new RFFIT laboratory
was designed to meet stringent safety, quality and GMP standards. Prior to receiving the FDA approval, the laboratory was also approved
by Health Canada and the Israeli Ministry of Health.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">The RFFIT test is used by Kamada to quantify the
Anti-Rabies potency throughout all key steps of KEDRAB and KAMRAB manufacturing from plasma collection and sourcing to final product release.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">In 2025, Kamada&rsquo;s sales of KEDRAB and KAMRAB
totaled over $70 million, representing the leading franchise in the company&rsquo;s portfolio of specialty plasma-derived products.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&ldquo;Our new state-of-the-art RFFIT laboratory
supports our continued progress as a leading global vertically integrated specialty plasma-derived company,&rdquo; said Amir London, Kamada&rsquo;s
Chief Executive Officer. &ldquo;The new laboratory will enable increased operational efficiencies through utilization of our in-house,
highly professional capabilities supported by external testing expertise. The establishment of this lab is also a demonstration of our
ongoing commitment to innovation, scientific excellence, and investment in advanced quality infrastructure.&quot;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><b>About Kamada</b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><b>&nbsp;</b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><b></b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Kamada Ltd. (the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;) is a global
biopharmaceutical company with a portfolio of marketed products indicated for rare and serious conditions and a leader in the specialty
plasma-derived therapies field. FIMI Opportunity Funds, the leading private equity firm in Israel, is the Company&rsquo;s controlling
shareholder, beneficially owning approximately 38% of the outstanding ordinary shares. The Company&rsquo;s strategy is focused on driving
profitable growth through four primary growth pillars: First, organic growth of its commercial portfolio, including continued investment
in the commercialization and life cycle management of its proprietary products, consisting of six FDA-approved specialty plasma-derived
products: KEDRAB&reg;, GLASSIA&reg;, CYTOGAM&reg;, VARIZIG&reg;, WINRHO SDF&reg; and HEPAGAM B&reg;, as well as KAMRAB&reg;, and two equine-based
anti-snake venom products. Second, distribution of third parties' pharmaceutical products in Israel &amp; the MENA region through in-licensing
partnerships, including the launch of several biosimilar products in Israel. Third, the Company is ramping up its plasma collection operations
to support revenue growth through the sale of normal source plasma to other plasma-derived manufacturers, and to support its increasing
demand for hyper-immune plasma. The Company currently owns three FDA approved operating plasma collection centers in the United States,
in Beaumont, Houston, and San Antonio, Texas. Fourth, the Company aims to secure new mergers and acquisitions, business development, in-licensing
and/or collaboration opportunities, which are anticipated to enhance the Company&rsquo;s marketed products portfolio and leverage its
financial strength and existing commercial infrastructure to drive long-term profitable growth. The Company is leveraging its manufacturing,
research and development expertise to advance the development and commercialization of additional product candidates, targeting areas
of significant unmet medical need.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</p>
