Item 3.
Source and Amount of Funds or Other Consideration
 
On September 7, 2023, FIMI Opportunity 7 and FIMI Israel Opportunity 7 (collectively, the “FIMI 7 Funds”) purchased, and Kamada issued and sold, an aggregate of 12,631,579 newly issued Ordinary Shares for a purchase price of $4.75 per Ordinary Share, or $60,000,000 in the aggregate, of which FIMI Opportunity 7 purchased 4,911,158 Ordinary Shares and FIMI Israel Opportunity 7 purchased 7,720,421 Ordinary Shares, upon consummation of the transactions set forth in the Share Purchase Agreement (the “Share Purchase Agreement”), dated May 23, 2023, by and among Kamada and the FIMI 7 Funds.
 
Item 5.
Interest in Securities of the Issuer
